RJU BCA — Complete Semester-wise Subject List

Programme: Bachelor in Computer Application (BCA), Rajarshi Janak University (RJU), Janakpurdham, Nepal
Structure recorded in the available RJU programme/curriculum materials: 4 years, 8 semesters, 128 credits.

Academic-year caution (2025–26 and 2026–27): This repository uses the subject tables recorded from the RJU BCA programme page and the available 2075 BS curriculum PDF. The PDF is an older curriculum document; the files available here do not establish that a separately revised syllabus for both 2025–26 and 2026–27 has been issued or that every course detail is unchanged. Confirm the applicable scheme and elective availability with RJU/your college before relying on it for registration or examinations.

Verification and known discrepancies

  • Subject tables and semester credit totals are recorded in the RJU BCA programme page and the available BCA curriculum PDF.
  • The curriculum PDF is dated 2075 BS and should not be treated as proof of a new 2025–26 or 2026–27 revision.
  • The Semester 2 Mathematics course appears as HMTH-153 in the semester table, while the detailed outline reportedly prints HCAC-153.
  • The exact title/decoding of HCANP-403 is not confirmed in the available material.
  • The Semester 5 HCAC-302 outline has a semester-label inconsistency; the semester table places it in Semester 5.
  • Elective pools list available options; the actual options offered may vary by college.
  • See Syllabus Verification Record and individual course README files for further details.

Scope

This is a student-maintained index based on the materials included in this repository. It is not an official RJU publication. Course outlines, assessment details, and the academic-year applicability must be confirmed with the university or college.
