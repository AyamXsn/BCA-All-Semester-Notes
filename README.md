## Academic-year and syllabus notice

The available subject tables are recorded from the [RJU BCA programme page](https://rju.edu.np/course/bca/) and the [BCA curriculum PDF](https://rju.edu.np/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/BCA-full-syllabus-1.pdf). The PDF is dated 2075 BS. This material does **not by itself confirm** that a revised syllabus for both 2025–26 and 2026–27 has been published or that every detail remains unchanged. Check with RJU or your college before using this index for course registration, exam preparation, or elective selection.

Known discrepancies and verification notes are documented in [Resources/Syllabus-Verification.md](Resources/Syllabus-Verification.md).

## How to use

1. Open [SUBJECT-LIST.md](SUBJECT-LIST.md) and select your semester.
2. Open the corresponding course folder.
3. Compare the course outline with the syllabus applicable to your intake.
4. Use the general computing notes and programming practice as supplementary material.
5. Add verified notes, practicals, and past papers to the matching course folder.

## Important disclaimer

This is an independent student resource, not an official university publication. Course folders are learning-resource placeholders unless they contain actual notes. Subject names and credit totals are based on the available RJU materials; academic-year applicability, course details, and elective availability should be confirmed with the university/college.
