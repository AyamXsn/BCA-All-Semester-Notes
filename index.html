A NOTE ABOUT THIS ARCHIVE

Useful, open,
and still growing.

This is an independent student resource for the Bachelor in Computer Application (BCA) programme at Rajarshi Janak University (RJU), Janakpurdham, Nepal. It is designed to make the available course structure and supplementary learning materials easier to navigate.

Curriculum note
The subject tables are based on the available RJU BCA programme page and curriculum PDF (dated 2075 BS). These sources do not independently confirm revisions for 2025–26 or 2026–27. Please verify current subjects, course details and elective availability with RJU or your college. Known discrepancies are listed in Syllabus Verification.

This is not an official university publication. Course folders may be learning-resource placeholders unless actual notes are present. See the repository README and LICENSE for further information.
