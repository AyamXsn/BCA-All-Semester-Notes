# HMTH-103 - Math-I

**Semester:** 1 (First Year) | **Credit hours:** 3 | **Full marks:** 60 + 40 (pass 24 + 16) | **Nature:** Theory + Practical

## Subject Introduction

This course gives the mathematical base for computer applications: number systems, relations and functions, matrices, progressions, counting methods and coordinate geometry. Practical demonstrations use MATLAB.

Source: official RJU curriculum outline ([PDF](https://rju.edu.np/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/BCA-full-syllabus-1.pdf), [programme page](https://rju.edu.np/course/bca/)), transcribed and condensed here.

## Syllabus Units

Allotted lecture hours listed in the outline: **45**.

| Unit | Title | Hours | Topics (condensed) | Notes status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | Real and Complex Number Systems | 7 | Number systems, real numbers and ordering, intervals, complex numbers, their geometry, algebra, conjugates and absolute value. | Not started |
| II | Relations and Functions | 6 | Cartesian product, relations and their properties, equivalence and partial order relations, Hasse diagrams, lattices, injective/surjective/bijective functions, composite and inverse functions, standard functions and graphs. | Not started |
| III | Matrix and Determinant | 7 | Types and algebra of matrices, transpose, symmetric and skew-symmetric matrices, determinants, minors, cofactors, adjoint and inverse, echelon form and rank, linear transformations. | Not started |
| IV | Progressions | 8 | Sequences, arithmetic/geometric/harmonic progressions and means, infinite series, partial sums, sum of an infinite geometric series. | Not started |
| V | Permutations and Combinations | 8 | Counting principles, factorials, linear and circular permutations, with and without repetition, combinations and their properties, restricted combinations. | Not started |
| VI | Coordinate Geometry | 9 | Conic sections (pair of lines, circle, ellipse, parabola, hyperbola), equations and graphs, 3D coordinates, distance and section formulae, direction cosines and ratios, planes. | Not started |
