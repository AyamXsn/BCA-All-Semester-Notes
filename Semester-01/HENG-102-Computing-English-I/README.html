HENG-102 - Foundation Course in Computing English-I

Semester: 1 (First Year) | Credit hours: 3 | Full marks: 60 + 40 (pass 24 + 16) | Nature: Theory

Subject Introduction

This course builds the English skills needed to study and work in computing. Each unit pairs a computing topic with a language focus, such as word formation, comparisons, the passive voice and cause and effect.

Source: official RJU curriculum outline (PDF, programme page), transcribed and condensed here.
