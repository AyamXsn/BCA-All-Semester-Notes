# HCAC-101 - Computer Fundamental and Office Automation

**Semester:** 1 (First Year) | **Credit hours:** 3 | **Full marks:** 60 + 20 + 20 (pass 24 + 8 + 8) | **Nature:** Theory + Lab

## Subject Introduction

This course introduces how computers work and how to use everyday software. It covers computer basics and generations, storage and I/O devices, software types, databases and security, networking and the Internet, and practical work with operating systems, Office tools and Linux.

Source: official RJU curriculum outline ([PDF](https://rju.edu.np/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/BCA-full-syllabus-1.pdf), [programme page](https://rju.edu.np/course/bca/)), transcribed and condensed here.

## Syllabus Units

Allotted lecture hours listed in the outline: **45**.

| Unit | Title | Hours | Topics (condensed) | Notes status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | Computers Overview | 5 | Characteristics and evolution of computers, generations, block diagram (input, storage, output, ALU, control unit, CPU), applications, capabilities and limitations. | Not started |
| II | Storage Fundamentals | 8 | Primary vs secondary storage, RAM, ROM, PROM, EPROM, EEPROM, registers, buses, cache, magnetic tapes and disks, optical disks, flash drives. | Not started |
| III | Input/Output Devices | 9 | Role of I/O devices, sequential and direct-access devices, keyboards, pointing devices, scanners, voice and vision input, touch screens, monitors, printers, plotters, sound cards, speakers. | Not started |
| IV | Software | 9 | Machine, assembly and high-level languages, types of software, system software, language processors, application and open-source software, operating system roles, types and structure. | Not started |
| V | DBMS and Computer Security | 5 | File systems vs DBMS, types of DBMS, security threats, viruses and their types, solutions, e-waste disposal and recycling. | Not started |
| VI | Communication System | 9 | Elements of communication, transmission modes, speed and media, analog and digital transmission, topologies, LAN/WAN/MAN, OSI model, Internet concepts, TCP/IP, WWW, IP address, URL, domain names, ISPs, social media risks, Internet security, search engines, intranet. | Not started |
