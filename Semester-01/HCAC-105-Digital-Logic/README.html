HCAC-105 - Digital Logic

Semester: 1 (First Year) | Credit hours: 3 | Full marks: 60 + 20 + 20 (pass 24 + 8 + 8) | Nature: Theory + Lab

Subject Introduction

This course covers the design of digital circuits: number systems and codes, Boolean algebra and logic gates, K-map simplification, combinational and sequential circuits, registers and counters, with emphasis on design and simulation.

Source: official RJU curriculum outline (PDF, programme page), transcribed and condensed here.

Syllabus Units

Allotted lecture hours listed in the outline: 45.

Unit Title Hours Topics (condensed) Notes status
I Binary Systems 6 Digital signals, number systems and conversions, complements, signed numbers, binary codes (BCD, Excess-3, Gray, 8421), binary arithmetic, error detection and correction codes. Not started
II Boolean Algebra and Logic Gates 5 Boolean theorems and functions, logic gates, realisation using universal gates, timing diagrams, integrated circuits. Not started
III Simplification of Boolean Functions 5 Karnaugh maps (2, 3, 4 variables), SOP and POS, NAND/NOR implementation, don't-care conditions. Not started
IV Combinational Logic 12 Analysis and design, adders, subtractors, code converters, multilevel NAND/NOR, comparators, decoders, encoders, multiplexers, ROM, PLA, PAL. Not started
V Sequential Logic 9 Latches, flip-flops, triggering, excitation tables, flip-flop conversion, clocked sequential circuits, state reduction and assignment, intro to asynchronous logic. Not started
VI Registers and Counters 8 Registers, shift registers, universal registers, ripple and synchronous counters, timing sequences, digital watch application. Not started
