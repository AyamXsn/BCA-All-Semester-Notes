## Verification rules
1. Prefer official RJU publications or official department communications.
2. Check the curriculum's applicability to the student's admission batch.
3. Transcribe course titles and units accurately.
4. Do not import another university's syllabus.
5. Record discrepancies between official documents rather than silently reconciling them.
6. Update semester and subject folders only after evidence is recorded.

## Content classification
- **RJU-specific:** only facts supported by a cited official RJU source.
- **General supplementary:** original computing explanations and examples; not a claim about RJU subject placement.
