# Study Guidelines

1. Obtain the syllabus for your own admission batch and follow its learning outcomes.
2. Make a weekly timetable and distribute effort across subjects.
3. For theory, learn precise definitions, then explain concepts in your own words.
4. Draw diagrams from memory and label every component.
5. For programming, type code manually, predict output, execute, and debug.
6. Practise tracing algorithms and calculating time/space complexity.
7. Build small projects that combine concepts.
8. Use genuine past papers only when their source is known.
9. Treat generated questions as practice, not exam predictions.
10. Review errors and weak topics every week.
11. Practise timed answers and allocate time according to marks.
12. Ask instructors to resolve ambiguity in course requirements.
